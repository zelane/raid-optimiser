Chimera trials

Pick the rotation (the column holding its mythical materials), put the champions you want in the team's slots, then fill the open slots. The trials the team can clear in one Ultra Nightmare fight light up: Easy, then Normal, then Hard, in all three forms. A team qualifies on the buffs, debuffs and mechanics its skills can bring, against each trial's rules and each form's immunities. Damage isn't checked (the targets are shown), nor skill chances, turn order or gear sets.
