Chimera trials

Teams of five that can clear one column of trials (Easy, then Normal, then Hard) in all three of the Chimera's forms in a single Ultra Nightmare fight: pick the column holding this rotation's mythical materials. A team qualifies on the buffs, debuffs and mechanics its skills can bring, against what each trial's rules check and what each form is immune to. Damage isn't checked (the targets are shown), nor skill chances, turn order or gear sets: the plan says who can do each trial, not that they will.
