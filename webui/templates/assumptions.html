Glyphs

Every substat of a kind chosen here is taken as carrying that glyph at its top roll, unless the glyph already on it is better. Gear below 6★ takes glyphs up to its own rank, so there it's capped at that rank's top roll. A solve only glyphs stats it wants more of: a minimum, a priority, or a stat the ranked skill's damage uses (and C.DMG); a stat with only a maximum isn't glyphed. Main stats can't be glyphed, and the game has no C.RATE or C.DMG glyphs.
